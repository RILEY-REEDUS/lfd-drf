Critical driving behaviours with Driver’s Risk Field

Yurui Du1,2, Flavia Sofia Acerbo2, Jens Kober1, and Tong Duy Son2

1 Department of Cognitive Robotics, Delft University of Technology, Delft, the Netherlands
2 Siemens Digital Industries Software, Leuven, Belgium

This work uses Driver’s Risk Field to generate safety-critical simulation scenarios and augment demonstrations for data-efficient imitation learning in autonomous driving.

Generated critical scenarios expose harder failure cases.

DRF-based traffic agents generate critical interactions that reveal failures not exposed by the ordinary logged scenario. These side-by-side videos show how a critical scenario can turn a hidden weakness into an observable failure.

Critical-data augmentation reduces passive driving.

Critical data augmentation targets passive-driving failure modes: before retraining, the policy drives passively; after retraining, it performs a more normal speed-up in the same type of interaction.
